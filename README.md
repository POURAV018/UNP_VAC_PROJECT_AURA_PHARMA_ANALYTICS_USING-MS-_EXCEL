# UNP_VAC_PROJECT_AURA_PHARMA_ANALYTICS_USING_MS_EXCEL

Project Scope: An end-to-end pharmaceutical distribution analytics and econometric forecasting model for Aura Therapeutics India Ltd., developed in collaboration with UNP Education across 7,120+ unique shipment batch records.

Data Preprocessing & Engineering: Built a reproducible Microsoft Excel pipeline covering duplicate batch removal, missing-value imputation, IQR outlier detection, binary mapping, and dummy variable encoding across 20 operational and clinical features.

Statistical Hypothesis Testing (𝛼=0.05): Conducted parametric validation using paired and independent two-sample 𝑡t-tests, a one-sample 𝑍-test, and One-Way ANOVA across distribution channels, therapeutic segments, and pricing tiers.

Econometric Modeling & Dashboards: Designed multivariate KPI dashboards and estimated OLS Simple and Multiple Linear Regression models, achieving (𝑅-square = 0.897) for quarterly physical strip demand and (𝑅-square = 0.720) for wholesale billing revenue.

Key Business Findings:

Medical representative detailing campaigns drive a statistically significant +25.17% lift in monthly prescriptions (+606.5 slips/territory).

Active wholesale trade schemes increase quarterly stockist dispatch volume by +24.09%.

Extended C&F warehouse hold times erode wholesale billing revenue by -₹0.847 Lakhs per hold day, highlighting a high-impact working capital optimization lever.
